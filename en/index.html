Built for the big camera

Keepsight was made for one particular rig: a strong gimbal carries a mirrorless camera, for example a Lumix S1RII filming in 8K. The iPhone sits on top. It detects the subject and steers the gimbal, while the camera underneath takes the actual footage.

You get a tracked shot in the quality of your best camera, through your own lenses, without panning by hand and without a second person on the gimbal.

Photos and videos from the iPhone itself are a bonus: a second angle, a reference picture, or for the days the big camera stays at home.

What Keepsight does for you

Follow

Choose a subject: person, face, aircraft, bird, animal, vehicle, train or boat. Keepsight keeps it centred and steers the gimbal. If a cloud hides the aircraft, the app briefly keeps following its path.

Zoom

Auto zoom keeps the subject at the size you set. It moves in tiny steps and stays within the selected lens, so the video never jumps.

Frame

The virtual gimbal saves a second video whose frame follows the subject smoothly and zooms by itself. It works handheld too, without a gimbal.

Shoot

With Keepsight Pro the app takes the photo by itself when everyone smiles, when everyone looks at the camera, or when the subject is large enough in the frame. Interval series, hand gestures and voice commands with a countdown are already part of the free version.

Questions and answers

Do I pay once or regularly?

Once. Keepsight Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no recurring costs.

What can I do for free?

Follow people and faces, use gestures, voice commands, auto zoom and the virtual gimbal, and film videos of up to 60 seconds with the Keepsight logo. You can also try Pro tracking three times for 60 seconds.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, open the Pro screen and choose to restore the purchase. You must be signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Why does the price differ in my country?

Apple sets the price per country and currency, including taxes. The price shown in your App Store is the one that applies.

Does Keepsight work without internet?

Yes. Detection and tracking run on the iPhone. Internet is needed only for the purchase and for the optional flight data.

What happens to my pictures?

They stay on your iPhone. Keepsight has no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Does Keepsight record with my big camera?

No. Keepsight steers the gimbal; the camera on it records on its own. Keepsight's own photos and videos come from the iPhone camera.

Is live flight data available where I live?

Only in supported countries and regions, and it needs location access and internet. At present no region is approved.

What you need

iPhone

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Keepsight is developed and tested on the iPhone 15 Pro Max with three cameras.

Gimbal

For following, a gimbal or stand with Apple DockKit, such as the Insta360 Flow 2 Pro, DJI Osmo Mobile 8 or Belkin Auto-Tracking Stand Pro. The FeiyuTech AK4500 for mirrorless cameras is supported as a beta. Camera, photo triggers, gestures, voice and the virtual gimbal also work without a gimbal.

Languages

The app speaks 21 languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).
