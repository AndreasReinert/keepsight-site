Put the iPhone down. Keepsight films you.

Stand it on a table, a tripod or a gimbal and step into the picture. Keepsight keeps you in the frame while you move.

Read the documentationFilming people is free and unlimited. Keepsight is currently in beta testing and not yet available on the App Store.

How it works

  1. Put it down

    Lean the iPhone against something, use a tripod or place it in a gimbal.

  2. Step into the picture

    Keepsight recognises you and follows you. Without a gimbal, the frame of the video moves with you.

  3. Record and share

    Press record and share the finished video straight from the app.

No gimbal? No problem.

Keepsight is a complete camera app even without a gimbal, handheld or on a small tripod. Made for vlogs, selfies and group photos.

Auto capture

Put the iPhone down and step into the picture. With Keepsight Pro the app takes the photo by itself when everyone smiles or looks at the camera.

Dual capture

Film what happens in front of you and your own reaction at the same time. With Keepsight Pro your selfie appears as a small picture in the video.

Virtual gimbal

Without any motor, Keepsight saves a second video whose frame follows the subject smoothly and zooms by itself.

Hands-free

Start a video or take a photo with a hand gesture or your voice, with a countdown so everyone is ready.

Full control over the camera

With Keepsight Pro you set exposure, ISO, shutter, white balance, tint and focus yourself, each one separately. Whatever you leave alone stays automatic.

Sound as good as the picture

Keepsight shows your sound level before and while you record and warns you when you are too quiet, too loud or distorted. With Keepsight Pro you choose the microphone, record your voice through AirPods or a wireless microphone, and capture spatial audio on the iPhone models that support it.

What Keepsight does for you

Follow

Choose a subject: person, face, aircraft, bird, animal, vehicle, train or boat. Keepsight keeps it centred and steers the gimbal. If a cloud hides the aircraft, the app briefly keeps following its path.

Zoom

Auto zoom keeps the subject at the size you set. It moves in tiny steps and stays within the selected lens, so the video never jumps.

Frame

The virtual gimbal saves a second video whose frame follows the subject smoothly and zooms by itself. It works handheld too, without a gimbal.

Shoot

With Keepsight Pro the app takes the photo by itself when everyone smiles, when everyone looks at the camera, or when the subject is large enough in the frame. Interval series, hand gestures and voice commands with a countdown are already part of the free version.

Questions and answers

Do I need a gimbal?

No. Camera, auto capture, dual capture, gestures, voice commands and the virtual gimbal work handheld or on a tripod. A gimbal adds real camera movement.

Do I pay once or regularly?

Once. Keepsight Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no recurring costs.

What can I do for free?

Follow people and faces without a time limit, with gestures, voice commands, the virtual gimbal and any DockKit gimbal. Videos are saved in 1080p with a small logo. You can also try Pro three times for 3 minutes.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, open the Pro screen and choose to restore the purchase. You must be signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Why does the price differ in my country?

Apple sets the price per country and currency, including taxes. The price shown in your App Store is the one that applies.

Does Keepsight work without internet?

Yes. Detection and tracking run on the iPhone. Internet is needed only for the purchase.

What happens to my pictures?

They stay on your iPhone. Keepsight has no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Does Keepsight record with my big camera?

No. Keepsight steers the gimbal; the camera on it records on its own. Keepsight's own photos and videos come from the iPhone camera.

Is live flight data available where I live?

Only in approved countries and regions, and it needs your own ADS-B receiver and location access. At present no region is approved.

Can Keepsight follow fast birds?

Not always. Very fast or erratic animals can be quicker than the gimbal can turn or than the iPhone can recognise them.

What you need

iPhone

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Keepsight is developed and tested on the iPhone 15 Pro Max and iPhone 17 Pro Max with three cameras.

Gimbal

For following, a gimbal or stand with Apple DockKit, such as the Insta360 Flow 2 Pro, DJI Osmo Mobile 8 or Belkin Auto-Tracking Stand Pro. The FeiyuTech AK4500 for mirrorless cameras is supported as well. Camera, photo triggers, gestures, voice and the virtual gimbal also work without a gimbal.

Languages

The app speaks 21 languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Swedish, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Thai, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).
