Keepsight: help and support

Keepsight keeps your subject in sight: it detects people, aircraft, animals and more, and steers your gimbal to follow them. All detection runs on your iPhone.

Contact
Questions, bugs or wishes: ar.orca@gmail.com
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Keepsight version (in the app under All settings → About and licenses).

Frequently asked questions

Which gimbal do I need?

Keepsight currently controls the FeiyuTech AK4500 over Bluetooth. Camera, photos, auto capture, voice and gesture control and the virtual gimbal also work without a gimbal.

The gimbal is not found

Switch the gimbal on, close the Feiyu app and allow Bluetooth for Keepsight (Settings → Keepsight → Bluetooth). Then tap “Connect” in Keepsight.

The gimbal turns the wrong way

Under All settings → Direction you can invert pan and tilt.

How do I change the language?

In the app under All settings → Language. This opens the iPhone settings for Keepsight, where you choose the language.

Voice commands do not work

Allow the microphone and speech recognition (Settings → Keepsight). The commands are “photo”, “start video” and “stop” in the app’s language.

Where are my photos and videos?

In the Photos app on your iPhone and in the Keepsight gallery (thumbnail at the bottom left of the viewfinder).

I bought Keepsight Pro but it is not active

Tap PRO → “Restore purchase”. You must be signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
