Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. Applies to the Keepsight iOS app and this website.

In short: Keepsight does not collect data about you. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. Camera images, detection, photos and videos stay on your iPhone.

2. What the app processes

The app processes data only on your device and only to provide its features. I, as the provider, receive none of it.

  • Camera: The camera image is analysed on the iPhone to detect and follow subjects. Images do not leave the device.
  • Microphone: for the sound of your videos and, if you turn on voice control, for voice commands.
  • Speech recognition: Voice commands are recognised by Apple’s speech recognition. If your iPhone and the selected language support it, this happens on the device. Otherwise the audio is sent to Apple for recognition, subject to Apple’s privacy policy. Voice control is off by default.
  • Photos: The app saves your captures to the photo library and shows only captures made with Keepsight in its gallery. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple transfers them according to your iCloud settings.
  • Bluetooth: to control your gimbal. Only control commands and status values are exchanged with the gimbal.
  • Settings: Your in-app settings are stored locally on the iPhone only.

3. Purchases

The purchase of “Keepsight Pro” is handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no payment details, name or email address, only aggregated sales figures from Apple.

4. Test versions (TestFlight)

If you use a test version through TestFlight, Apple processes your email address for the invitation and, if you agree, crash reports and your feedback. I see feedback and crash reports in order to fix problems, and the email addresses of testers I invited myself.

5. Contact by email

If you write to me, I use your email address and the content of your message only to reply. I delete messages once the matter is resolved, after two years at the latest.

6. This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

7. Your rights

Under the Swiss Data Protection Act and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Switzerland the FDPIC). Because the app sends no data to me, in practice this concerns only emails you have sent me. Write to ar.orca@gmail.com.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from them.

9. Changes

If the app changes in a way that requires changes to this policy, it will be updated here. The version on this page applies.
