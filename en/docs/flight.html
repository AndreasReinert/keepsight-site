Flight data

Live flight data in the video: where it is available, consent, sources.

Availability: Live flight data is available only in supported countries and regions; location access and internet are required. At present no region is approved, so the feature stays switched off everywhere.

With the flight data overlay, Keepsight shows the callsign, aircraft type, altitude and speed of the aircraft you are following, in the viewfinder and in the recorded video. The data comes from adsb.lol, a community network of ADS-B receivers.

Switching it on

  1. Open the settings and switch on Flight data overlay (ADS-B).
  2. Read the notice and tap Agree and enable. Only then does iOS ask for your location.
  3. Choose Aircraft as the subject and point the camera at the aircraft.

If several aircraft fit the direction the camera is looking, Keepsight does not guess: tap Aircraft selection required and choose the right one.

Where it works

Keepsight determines the country on your iPhone from your current location. Your App Store country, language or internet address play no part. Near a border and at sea the feature stays off.

Every country is reviewed individually before it is approved. Until then the app tells you that live flight data is unavailable in your region. Tracking and recording work as usual.

What is sent

Only while the feature is active does Keepsight send your location, rounded to about one kilometre, to adsb.lol to find nearby aircraft. Nothing is sent before you agree, and nothing in regions that are not approved.

Display

Choose the corner, compact or detailed, and metric or imperial units. Data older than 15 seconds is hidden. Every video with flight data carries the credit “Data: adsb.lol · ODbL 1.0”.

The overlay is written into videos recorded with the iPhone. A camera on the gimbal records on its own and gets no overlay.

Accuracy

Flight data may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. It is for information only and not suitable for navigation, flight safety or operational decisions.
