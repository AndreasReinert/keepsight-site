Recording and gallery

Which videos are created, where they are and how to view them.

Recording video

In video mode the red shutter starts and stops the recording. The running time appears at the top centre in a red field, in place of the format. Videos have sound and are saved to the Photos app.

Which videos are created

VideoContentWhen
OriginalThe clean camera image in the selected resolution.Always, unless you switch it off for the virtual gimbal.
Virtual gimbalThe following frame in 1080p.When the virtual gimbal is on.
Overlay videoThe image with frames, time, subject, status and gimbal data in 1080p.With Keepsight Pro, when Overlay video is on.

Gallery

The thumbnail above the mode switch shows the latest capture. Tap it to open the gallery with all photos and videos you made with Keepsight, newest first.

After a photo the viewfinder darkens briefly and the new picture jumps into the thumbnail. A message only appears if something goes wrong.

Videos with highlights show stars with the time under the video; a tap jumps to the marked moment.

  • Tap a picture for the large view and swipe to browse.
  • Date, megapixels and image size are shown at the top.
  • The bin deletes the capture from the Photos app as well. The iPhone asks first.

To delete several at once, tap Select at the top left, tap the photos and videos you want, or Select all, then the bin at the bottom. The iPhone asks once for all of them.

The gallery needs read access to Photos. With “Limited access”, Keepsight still sees its own captures.

If a tile shows a cloud symbol, the original is in iCloud and is being downloaded. This happens when iCloud Photos is on with “Optimise iPhone Storage”. A warning sign means the file cannot be read; the large view then shows the file name.
