Settings and help

Language, help mode and the controls for advanced users.

The most important switches are in the two side panels. Everything else is in the left panel under All settings.

Help mode

The question mark at the top of each side panel switches help mode on. Yellow hints with a short explanation then appear under the menu items, with a diagram for the important ones.

Language

Keepsight uses the language of your iPhone. To choose a different one for Keepsight only, tap Language under All settings. This opens the iPhone settings for Keepsight. The voice commands change with it.

Controls for advanced users

SettingMeaning
Speed “Custom”Gain, damping, top speed, acceleration and calm zone of the tracking.
Minimum speedFor the AK4500 only: the smallest command the gimbal still reacts to. Raise it if the gimbal stops at small deviations.
Prediction after lossHow long the gimbal keeps panning when the subject is hidden.
DirectionInvert pan or tilt.
Detection modelWhich model detects a subject. RF-DETR Small and YOLOX-S are available.
Developer infoShows frame rate, gimbal commands and model timing in the viewfinder.
Reset to defaultsRestores the tracking controls.
