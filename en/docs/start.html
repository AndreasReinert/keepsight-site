Getting started

Permissions, connecting the gimbal, your first tracking.

Permissions

On first launch the iPhone asks for access. Keepsight only asks for each one when it is needed.

AccessWhat for
CameraDetecting and filming subjects. The app does not work without the camera.
BluetoothControlling the gimbal.
PhotosSaving captures and showing them in the gallery. “Limited access” is enough.
MicrophoneSound for videos and voice commands.
Speech recognitionVoice commands only.
LocationLive flight data only: checking your region and recognising the aircraft. Stays on the iPhone.
Local networkLive flight data only: connecting to your own ADS-B receiver.

If you declined an access, you can allow it later under Settings → Keepsight.

Connecting the gimbal

  • Gimbal or stand with Apple DockKit: Pair the device with your iPhone once, as the manufacturer describes. After that, Keepsight connects by itself when you mount the iPhone.
  • FeiyuTech AK4500: Switch the gimbal on and tap the yellow gimbal symbol at the bottom right in Keepsight. This works in the full view only.

Once connected, the gimbal symbol turns green. Details are in the chapter Supported gimbals.

The gimbal must be able to turn freely. Keep fingers, cables and objects out of its range of movement.

Your first tracking

In simple mode there is nothing to set up: step in front of the camera and press record. The steps below are for the full view.

  1. Open the left panel and choose the subject Person.
  2. Step in front of the camera. A frame appears around you and the status at the top left reads “Target locked”.
  3. Tap the tracking button at the bottom right. It turns green and the gimbal follows you. Tap it again to stop.

If the gimbal turns away from the subject instead of towards it, Keepsight notices it after a moment and reverses the direction by itself. You can also invert an axis under All settings → Direction.
