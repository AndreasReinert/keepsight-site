Camera and format

Video and photo mode, resolution, frame rate, lenses.

Video or photo

The switch to the left of the shutter changes the mode. The camera reconfigures itself, as in Apple’s Camera app.

  • Video mode: continuous zoom across all three lenses, and auto zoom.
  • Photo mode: the full photo resolution of the selected lens. The viewfinder image is 4:3.

Setting the format

Tap the format display at the top centre. The panel is locked during a recording.

Manual controls

Manual controls: set ISO, shutter, white balance, tint and focus yourself, each one separately. Requires Keepsight Pro.

To open them, tap the format display at the top and switch Controls to Pro. You also find Manual controls in the camera panel; from there it also works while a video is recording.

The window is low and does not darken the picture, so you see every change at once. A small “A” marks each value the camera still sets itself. As soon as you move a slider, that value is fixed. Auto hands it back to the camera, All auto resets everything. Your values apply to photo and video and are kept until you change them.

New to this? Leave everything on Auto and try Exposure first. Every setting stays automatic until you move its slider. Auto hands it back to the camera.

Exposure

Exposure: makes the whole picture brighter or darker while the camera keeps measuring the light itself. The easiest control to start with.

Use it when the picture comes out too dark or too bright, for example a subject against a bright sky, snow or a stage. Plus makes it brighter, minus darker, in thirds of a stop from −3 to +3. It also works while ISO or shutter are fixed.

ISO

ISO: how sensitive the sensor is. A higher value makes the picture brighter but grainier. Keep it as low as the light allows.

Choose a low ISO in daylight for the cleanest picture and raise it indoors or at dusk. If you fix only the ISO, Keepsight adjusts the shutter time by itself so that the brightness stays right.

Shutter

Shutter: how long each picture is exposed. Short times such as 1/1000 freeze fast movement. Longer times let in more light but blur movement.

Use 1/1000 or shorter for birds, sport and aircraft so that they stay sharp. For natural-looking video, use about twice the frame rate, for example 1/60 at 30 frames per second. If you fix only the shutter, Keepsight adjusts the ISO by itself. In video the time cannot be longer than one frame; in photo mode it goes up to 1/4 second.

White balance

White balance: matches the colours to the light so that white looks white. Tap the symbol for lamp light, sun, cloud or shade, or set the value in Kelvin.

Fix it when the colours change during a recording, for example when the camera pans from sun into shade, or when lamp light looks too yellow. The symbols set 3200 K for light bulbs, 4000 K for fluorescent light, 5600 K for sun, 6500 K for a cloudy sky and 7500 K for shade. The slider covers 2500 K to 10000 K.

Tint

Tint: shifts the colours between green and magenta. Usually only needed under artificial light.

Use it when skin or white walls look greenish or pinkish, which often happens under LED or fluorescent lamps. Move the slider until white looks neutral again. While white balance is fixed and tint is on Auto, tint keeps the value measured last, and the other way round.

Focus

Focus: sets the distance that is sharp, from near on the left to far on the right.

Use it when the autofocus hunts, for example through a fence, a window or branches, or with a small subject against the sky. Set the distance once and it stays there. Distant subjects such as aircraft are at the right end of the slider.

Aperture

Aperture: the opening of the lens. Keepsight shows the value but cannot change it yet, not even on the iPhone 18 Pro.

Grid

Grid: two lines each way help you keep the horizon level and place your subject. They are not saved in photos or videos.

Switch it on in the format panel under Grid. It is available to everyone, also without Pro. Place your subject on one of the four crossing points for a balanced picture, and use the lines to keep the horizon and buildings straight.

Focus: AF and following focus

AF: the button AF at the end of the lens bar focuses once on the tracked subject, or on the centre of the picture when nothing is tracked. Free for everyone.

Tap to focus: tap anywhere in the viewfinder. Keepsight focuses and exposes there once, shown by a yellow square, and selects the subject you tapped. If you set focus or exposure by hand, that setting stays. Free for everyone.

Focus follows the subject: while Keepsight tracks a subject, the focus stays on it, even when it moves to the edge of the picture. Switch it on in the settings under Monitoring → Focus follows the subject. It is paused as soon as you set the focus by hand. Requires Keepsight Pro.

Monitoring tools

Monitoring tools show you what the picture really looks like, the way professional cameras do. They appear only on the screen and are never saved in photos or videos. Switch them on in the settings under Monitoring. Requires Keepsight Pro.

Quick switches: open the camera menu with the round button on the right. Under Monitoring you switch each tool on and off with one tap. Tap the frame tile again to change the format.

ToolWhat it shows
ZebraWhite diagonal stripes on areas that are too bright and lose detail. Set the threshold with Zebra level, from 75 to 100 %. If you see stripes on a face or the sky, lower the exposure.
Focus peakingColours the edges that are sharp. Useful with manual focus: turn the focus until your subject lights up. Choose the colour that stands out best: green, red, blue or yellow.
HistogramHow the brightness is spread, from dark on the left to bright on the right. A mountain pressed against the right edge means overexposure, against the left edge underexposure.
Audio metersThe sound level of each microphone channel from −45 to +3 dB, green, yellow from −12 dB, red from −3 dB. Visible while the microphone runs, that is during recording or with voice control.
Frame guideDarkens everything outside the chosen format: 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 4:5 and 1:1 for posts, 4:3, 16:9, and the cinema formats 1.85:1 and 2.39:1. The video itself keeps its full format, so you can crop it later.

Dual capture: video with selfie picture

Dual capture: the rear camera films and tracks your subject, the front camera films you at the same time. Your selfie appears as a small picture at the bottom right of the video.

Switch it on in the settings under Dual capture. With Save selfie video separately your selfie is also saved as its own video, so you can edit it yourself later. While dual capture is on, the viewfinder shows your selfie at the top right.

  • Both cameras record at up to 1080p with 30 frames per second, without 4K or ProRes. The rear camera uses the main lens, zoom is digital.
  • Tracking, gestures, voice commands and the gimbal keep working.
  • Only in video mode. Switching to photo mode or to the front camera ends dual capture.
  • If the iPhone gets hot, the selfie picture switches off by itself.
  • Requires Keepsight Pro and an iPhone that can film with both cameras at once. If yours cannot, Keepsight tells you.

Heat saver while recording

Long recordings in the sun make every iPhone warm. With Save heat while recording Keepsight saves energy during a recording and gives everything back when you stop. It is on by default and free for everyone.

  • The screen dims after 10 seconds. Tap it to wake it. The recording continues.
  • While your subject stands still, detection runs less often. As soon as it moves, it runs at full speed again.
  • Gestures are checked less often, voice control stays on.
  • Monitoring tools and the live view on the Apple Watch update less often.

If the iPhone gets warm anyway, Keepsight goes one step further: first the selfie picture switches off, then the overlay video pauses, then detection slows down. When the iPhone is too hot, tracking stops and the recording is saved.

Front camera and selfies

The button with the two arrows next to the thumbnail switches to the front camera. You see yourself mirrored, as in Apple’s Camera app. Captures are saved unmirrored, the way others see you; for photos you can change that.

  • Video and photo: video up to 4K, photos at the full resolution of the front camera.
  • Subject: when you switch, Keepsight changes to Face and aims at your eyes. With a gimbal it follows you in front of the front camera too.
  • Selfie bar in photo mode: above the shutter you choose Single, Smile or Look. Smile and Look are armed as soon as you tap them: Keepsight takes the photo by itself when everyone smiles or looks at the camera. Both are part of Keepsight Pro.
  • Countdown: with the front camera the screen flashes instead of the light.
  • Not available: lens selection and auto zoom, because the front camera has a single lens.

Portrait

Portrait in the selfie bar or in the camera panel is part of Keepsight Pro.

  • Photo: Keepsight saves the picture with the front camera’s depth data. Afterwards you adjust the background blur in the Photos app. Portrait photos are HEIF at 12 MP; ProRAW is not possible here.
  • Video: Keepsight chooses a format that supports Apple’s Portrait effect, usually 1080p at 30 frames. You switch the effect itself on in Control Center under Video effects; Keepsight opens it for you. Depending on your iPhone you also find Studio Light and Center Stage there.

Mirror selfies in the camera panel saves selfie photos the way you see yourself in the viewfinder.

The camera cannot be switched during a video recording.

Lenses and megapixels

LensPhoto resolution
.5× ultra wide12 MP; up to 48 MP from iPhone 16 Pro

Telephoto12 MP; up to 48 MP from iPhone 17 Pro

These are Apple’s specifications for the Pro models from iPhone 15 Pro. Keepsight has only been tested on the iPhone 15 Pro Max so far. Other models deliver fewer megapixels with some lenses. The format panel only ever shows what the current lens of your iPhone really delivers.

Triggered by voice, Keepsight switches to photo or video mode by itself; gestures work in the mode that is set. Only a photo taken while a video is recording stays at about 10 MP.
