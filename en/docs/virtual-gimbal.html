Virtual gimbal

A second video with a smoothly following, zooming frame.

The virtual gimbal cuts a frame out of the full camera image that follows the subject and zooms by itself. The result is a finished, calm video, available right after recording.

Turning it on

  1. Open the right panel and switch on Virtual gimbal.
  2. Choose a subject. In the viewfinder you see the frame as a bright outline; the area outside is dimmed.
  3. Start recording.

A real gimbal is not needed. You can hold the iPhone in your hand or put it on a tripod. Together with a gimbal and auto zoom the image becomes calmer still.

Settings

ControlEffect
Free space around subject10 to 40 percent. More space looks calmer, less space brings the subject closer.
Maximum zoom1.5× to 4×. When recording in 4K, full sharpness is kept up to 2×.
SmoothnessHow leisurely the frame follows. Larger values smooth more.
Also save originalSaves the full video alongside the framed one.

What is saved

  • The framed video in 1080p.
  • Optionally the original in the selected resolution.

If Keepsight loses the subject, the frame slowly returns to the full image.

The virtual gimbal keeps the subject steady in the frame. It does not compensate for strong shake of the background.
