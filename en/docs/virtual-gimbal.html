Virtual gimbal

A smoothly following frame, in 2K with Keepsight Pro.

The virtual gimbal cuts a frame out of the full camera image that follows the subject. The frame keeps its size, so the picture stays calm. The video is finished right after recording.

Turning it on

  1. Tap the gimbal symbol in the bar at the bottom until it is green and reads Virtual.
  2. Choose a subject. In the viewfinder you see the frame as a bright outline; the area outside is dimmed.
  3. Start recording.

A real gimbal is not needed. You can hold the iPhone in your hand or put it on a tripod. As soon as a gimbal is connected, the virtual gimbal rests: the gimbal follows and the full image is saved.

Settings

ControlEffect
Smooth, Standard, SportHow quickly the frame follows, in the right panel of the full view.
Also save originalSaves the full video alongside the framed one.

What is saved

  • The framed video: in 2K (2560 × 1440) with Keepsight Pro when you record in 4K, otherwise in 1080p.
  • Optionally the original in the selected resolution.

If Keepsight loses the subject, the frame slowly returns to the centre. If the subject no longer fits, the frame opens up.

The virtual gimbal suits little movement, such as vlogs, cooking or training on the spot. If you walk around the room, use a gimbal.

Image stabilization

Keepsight uses the image stabilization of iOS for videos: a stronger level with the virtual gimbal, the standard level otherwise. You can switch it off under All settings.
