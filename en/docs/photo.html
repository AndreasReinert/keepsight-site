Photos and triggers

Single shot, interval series and automatic capture at the right moment.

In photo mode, the right panel under Photo trigger sets how Keepsight takes pictures.

TypeWhat happens
SingleOne photo per press of the shutter.
IntervalA series at a fixed interval until the maximum number is reached.
Smile (Pro)Fires when all faces in the image are smiling with open eyes.
Look (Pro)Fires when everyone is facing the camera.
Subject (Pro)Fires when the selected subject is locked and large enough in the frame.

Single shot and interval are part of the free version. The three automatic types need Keepsight Pro; in the free version they show a lock.

Running a series

  1. Choose the trigger type.
  2. Set Interval and Maximum photos.
  3. Press the shutter. With the automatic types Keepsight is now armed and waits for the moment.
  4. The display at the top centre counts along, for example “3 / 30”. A second press of the shutter ends the series.
  • Interval: 1 to 30 seconds. With the automatic types it is the minimum gap between two photos.
  • Maximum photos: 2 to 100.

For birds and other animals, “Subject” with a short interval works well: Keepsight shoots as soon as the animal is large in the frame and keeps taking pictures while it stays there. The app does not detect where an animal is looking.

Photos in 48 MP and ProRAW take longer to save. With a very short interval the actual gap can therefore be longer.
