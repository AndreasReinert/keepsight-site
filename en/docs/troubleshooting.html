Troubleshooting

Common problems and how to solve them.

The gimbal is not found

  • Device with DockKit: Is it paired in the iPhone settings and is the iPhone seated properly in the mount? For comparison, open Apple’s Camera app. If the device does not follow you there, the pairing is the problem.
  • AK4500: Is the gimbal switched on and nearby? Close the Feiyu app completely and allow Bluetooth under Settings → Keepsight.
  • Switch the gimbal off and on again and tap Connect once more.

The gimbal turns away from the subject

Invert the affected axis under All settings → Direction.

The gimbal reacts sluggishly or shakes

  • Sluggish: choose the Sport profile. With the AK4500 a higher minimum speed also helps.
  • Shaking or swinging: choose the Smooth profile. With a long focal length small movements appear larger.

The subject is not detected

  • Is the right subject type selected?
  • Very small subjects are detected less reliably. Choose a longer lens.
  • Detection drops in low light and strong backlight.

Voice commands are not understood

  • Allow the microphone and speech recognition under Settings → Keepsight.
  • Speak the commands in the app’s language.
  • Wind and loud surroundings disturb recognition. Gestures are more reliable then.

Gestures do not trigger

  • The subject must be set to Person or Face.
  • Keep your hand still and clearly visible until the bar is full.

48 MP or RAW cannot be selected

Both are only available in photo mode with the main camera 1×. RAW also requires Keepsight Pro.

The gallery is empty

Allow Keepsight access to Photos. Captures from older versions of the app do not appear in the gallery, but they are in the Photos app.

Keepsight Pro is not active after purchase

Tap PRO → Restore purchase and check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account.

More help

Write to ar.orca@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Keepsight version.
