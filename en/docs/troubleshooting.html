Troubleshooting

Common problems and how to solve them.

The gimbal is not found

  • Device with DockKit: Is it paired in the iPhone settings and is the iPhone seated properly in the mount? For comparison, open Apple’s Camera app. If the device does not follow you there, the pairing is the problem.
  • AK4500: Is the gimbal switched on and nearby? Close the Feiyu app completely and allow Bluetooth under Settings → Keepsight.
  • Switch the gimbal off and on again and connect once more.

The gimbal turns away from the subject

Keepsight normally corrects this by itself after a moment. If not, invert the affected axis under All settings → Direction.

The gimbal reacts sluggishly or shakes

  • Sluggish: choose the Sport profile. With the AK4500 a higher minimum speed also helps.
  • Shaking or swinging: choose the Smooth profile. With a long focal length small movements appear larger.

The subject is not detected

  • Is the right subject type selected?
  • Very small subjects are detected less reliably. Choose a longer lens.
  • Detection drops in low light and strong backlight.

Voice commands are not understood

  • Allow the microphone and speech recognition under Settings → Keepsight.
  • Speak the commands in the app’s language.
  • For photo and video, say at least two words, for example “take a photo” or “start video”.
  • Wind and loud surroundings disturb recognition. Gestures are more reliable then.

Gestures do not trigger

  • The subject must be set to Person or Face.
  • Keep your hand still and clearly visible until the bar is full.
  • Only the tracked person can give gestures, or without tracking the largest person in the picture.
  • After a gesture, wait 2 seconds before the next one.
  • For ☝️ keep the thumb against your hand; for the L spread it out. That is how Keepsight tells the two apart.

48 MP or RAW cannot be selected

Both are only available in photo mode and with a lens that supports them. On the iPhone 15 Pro that is only the main camera 1×; newer Pro models also offer 48 MP with other lenses. 48 MP and RAW also require Keepsight Pro.

The gallery is empty

Allow Keepsight access to Photos. Captures from older versions of the app do not appear in the gallery, but they are in the Photos app.

Keepsight Pro is not active after purchase

Tap PRO → Restore purchase and check that you are signed in with the right Apple Account.

More help

Write to keepsight.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Keepsight version.

The iPhone gets hot

Detection, 4K recording and gimbal control are demanding, especially in the sun. Keepsight protects the device by itself:

  • Thermometer in the status field: the iPhone is hot and detection only evaluates every second or third frame. Tracking continues but reacts a little more slowly.
  • Tracking stopped: at a critical temperature Keepsight ends tracking and recording and saves the video. Let the iPhone cool down in the shade.

To keep it cooler: switch off detection and gesture control when you do not need them, 1080p instead of 4K, 30 instead of 60 frames, switch off the overlay video and virtual gimbal, remove the case. For this reason the iPhone 12 and older models start with 1080p.
