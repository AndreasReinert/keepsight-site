Tracking subjects

Subject types, custom target, speed and behaviour when the subject is hidden.

Choosing a subject

In the left panel you choose what Keepsight should look for.

SubjectVersionNote
PersonFreeThe whole body. Good for sports and movement.
FaceFreeFor close-ups, vlogs and selfies. Keepsight aims at the eyes: the eye line sits in the centre of the picture, not the nose.
AircraftProWorks best in the sky with the telephoto lens.
BirdPro
AnimalProDogs, cats, horses and more.
VehicleProCars, motorcycles, buses, trucks.
TrainPro
BoatPro

If several matching subjects are in the image, Keepsight takes the one nearest the centre. Tap another frame to switch.

Switching detection on and off

Detection takes computing power and warms the iPhone. Keepsight therefore only computes as much as needed:

  • Full rate: as long as tracking, auto zoom, the virtual gimbal, auto capture, gestures or a custom target need detection.
  • Preview: otherwise about five frames per second, so that you see the frames and can choose a target.
  • Off: the Detection switch at the top of the tracking panel turns it off completely. It starts by itself as soon as one of the functions above needs it.

The green frame always stays inside the visible picture, even when the subject is larger than the view.

Custom target

If you drag a frame around an object, Keepsight follows exactly that object, whatever the subject type. The left panel then shows the button Custom target. Tap it to clear the selection.

Speed

ProfileBehaviour
SmoothGentle start, half the top speed, small movements are ignored. For calm footage.
StandardBalanced. The right start for most subjects.
SportReacts fastest. For quick changes of direction.
CustomYou set all controller values yourself, see the Settings chapter.

When the subject disappears

  • Predicting: If the subject is hidden briefly, the gimbal keeps panning in the same direction. You set the duration under All settings; the default is 1.5 seconds.
  • Target lost: After that the gimbal stops until the subject is detected again.
