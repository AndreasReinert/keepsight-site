Gestures, voice, countdown

Operating the camera when you are in front of it yourself.

You find all three in the left panel under Remote control.

Hand gestures

Gestures work when the subject is set to Person or Face. Hold a gesture still for about half a second; a bar with the gesture’s icon fills at the top centre. Only the tracked person can give gestures, or without tracking the largest person in the picture. After a gesture, the next one is ignored for 2 seconds.

Keepsight understands five gestures. The first three are the same as on the DJI Osmo Mobile 8, the group gesture is the same as on the Insta360 Flow 2 Pro, and the highlight exists only in Keepsight.

GestureEffectVersion
✋Open handStart or stop tracking the person showing it.Free
✌️V signPhoto mode: take a photo. Video mode: start or stop the recording.Free
⌜⌟Double L with both hands (a frame), shown twiceAdjust the framing: centre the person and set auto zoom to the size of the frame.Pro
☝️Index finger upWhole group: everyone in the picture stays in view. Show it again to go back to the person showing it.Pro
👍Thumbs upHighlight: marks the current moment in the running recording.Pro

The V sign works in the mode that is set. To switch between photo and video hands-free, use a voice command.

What a highlight is

A highlight is a bookmark in the video. While a video is recording, show 👍 or tap the star on the Apple Watch, and Keepsight remembers that moment. Later, open the video in Keepsight’s gallery: the marks appear under the video as stars with the time, for example ★ 2:14. Tap one and the video jumps there, one second earlier so you do not miss the start.

Example: you film 20 minutes of training and the best jump is at 7:40. Show 👍 at that moment and find it again later with one tap.

The marks are shown only in Keepsight’s gallery, not in the Photos app or in editing programs, because they are stored in Keepsight and not in the video file. If you delete Keepsight, the marks are gone; the videos stay in Photos.

Keeping a group in the picture

☝️ draws one frame around everyone in the picture. Tracking and auto zoom keep the whole group in view, for example for a family, a band or a team. Show ☝️ again and Keepsight follows only the person showing it.

Handing over

Stop tracking with ✋ and for about five seconds anyone else can take over with ✋. Useful for interviews or when people take turns in front of the camera.

Gestures from further away

When the person giving gestures is small in the picture, Keepsight looks for hands only around that person. The hands then appear larger to the detection, so gestures also work from further away. How far depends on light and lens; this is still being measured.

Your own assignment

With Keepsight Pro you can give each gesture a different action in the settings, for example ✌️ always takes a photo, or a gesture does nothing. Reset gestures restores the standard.

Confirmation

Keepsight confirms every recognised gesture with the bar in the viewfinder, light and sound. If you wear an Apple Watch with Keepsight, it taps your wrist, so you know even with your back to the iPhone. The watch also has a page with all gestures.

Voice commands

Switch on Voice control and allow the microphone and speech recognition. Speak the commands in the app’s language.

You sayEffect
“Take a photo”Take a photo.
“Start video” or “Start recording”Start video recording.
“Stop”End the recording, the series or a running countdown.

Photo and video need at least two words, for example “make a video”, “start filming”, “take a picture” or “snap a photo”. A single word on its own triggers nothing, so normal conversation does not set off the camera. A single “stop” works only while a recording, a series or a countdown is running. If the camera is in the other mode, Keepsight switches by itself before it triggers.

While voice control is on, the microphone stays active. The iPhone shows this with the orange dot.

Countdown, flash and sound

When you trigger by gesture or voice, a countdown runs first so you can get into position.

  • Countdown: off, 3, 5 or 10 seconds. The seconds are shown large in the image.
  • Confirmation flash: the LED blinks at ever shorter intervals until the shot.
  • Confirmation sound: a short tick with every flash.

Without a countdown there is a single flash and tick as confirmation. The on-screen shutter always fires immediately.
