Auto zoom

Keeps the subject at the same size without the video jumping.

Auto zoom keeps the subject at the size you set. If it moves away, the camera slowly zooms in. If it comes closer, the camera zooms out.

Turning it on

  1. Switch to video mode.
  2. Select the lens whose range should be used.
  3. Tap A. The button shows the current zoom, for example “A 2.3×”.

Why the video does not jump

When an iPhone camera changes lenses while zooming, the image shifts visibly. Auto zoom therefore stays on the selected lens and moves only minimally with every single frame.

Selected lensZoom range
.5×0.5× to just under 1×
1×1× to just under 4×
5×5× to 10×

Settings

  • Subject size: how much of the image the subject should fill, 15 to 60 percent.
  • Zoom speed: how fast the zoom may move, 8 to 60 percent per second. Small values look calmer.

Behaviour in special cases

  • Zooming only starts once the subject has been detected steadily for just under a second.
  • If the subject reaches the edge of the image, the camera zooms out instead of in.
  • If the subject is hidden briefly, the zoom holds. Only after one and a half seconds without a subject does it slowly return.
