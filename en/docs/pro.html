Free and Pro

What the free version does, what Pro unlocks, purchase and restore.

The free version is for trying things out: you test following, gestures and the camera on people before paying anything.

FeatureFreePro
Track person and faceYesYes
Gestures, voice commands, countdownYesYes
Auto zoom and virtual gimbalYesYes
Gimbal control, single shot and interval seriesYesYes
Auto capture: smile, look, subjectNoYes
All other subjectsNoYes
Drag a frame around any objectNoYes
Video length60 secondsUnlimited
Pro tracking preview3 × 60 secondsUnlimited
Keepsight logo in photos and videosYesNo
Overlay videoNoYes
Photos in ProRAWNoYes

Trying Pro tracking

Tap a locked subject in the tracking panel and choose Start preview. For 60 seconds Keepsight follows this subject exactly as the Pro version does. You have three such sessions; one that fails within the first seconds does not count.

In the free version a video ends by itself after 60 seconds. A note appears 10 seconds before the end.

Buying Pro

Tap PRO at the top right and then Unlock. Pro is a one-time purchase without a subscription. The App Store shows the price in your currency and Apple handles the payment.

Restoring a purchase

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, tap PRO → Restore purchase. You must be signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.

Refunds

Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
