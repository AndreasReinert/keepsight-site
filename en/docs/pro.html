Free and Pro

What the free version does, what Pro unlocks, purchase and restore.

The free version films people in 1080p with a small Keepsight logo, without a time limit.

Simple mode

New installations start in simple mode: shutter, camera switch, photo and video, and the bar at the bottom with tracking and gimbal. Pro at the top right opens the full view with both side panels. Without a purchase you can look at everything there; whatever carries a lock needs Keepsight Pro. Simple mode below the green button takes you back.

The handle on the left opens a small menu with Person and Face, and below them the Pro subjects with a lock; tapping one offers a preview. The gear at the bottom right opens the short settings: language, help mode, documentation, feedback and legal information. Simple mode works with DockKit gimbals only.

Trying Pro tracking

Tap a locked subject in the tracking panel and choose Start preview. For 3 minutes Keepsight follows this subject exactly as the Pro version does. The time only runs while a subject is being followed, so waiting costs nothing. You have three such sessions; one that fails within the first seconds does not count. To test the FeiyuTech AK4500, touch and hold the gimbal symbol in the full view, choose FeiyuTech AK4500 and start a preview there.

If the iPhone’s storage is almost full, Keepsight does not start a recording, and it stops and saves a running recording in time.

Buying Pro

Tap PRO at the top right and then Unlock. Pro is a one-time purchase without a subscription. The App Store shows the price in your currency and Apple handles the payment.

Restoring a purchase

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, tap PRO → Restore purchase. You must be signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the purchase.

Have a code for Keepsight Pro? Tap PRO → Redeem code and enter it. Apple checks the code; it unlocks Pro just like a purchase.

Refunds

Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
