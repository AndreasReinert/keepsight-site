The viewfinder

The controls in the camera image and the three panels.

The viewfinder shows the camera image with almost no controls. Everything else sits in three panels that you bring in when needed.

In the viewfinder

ElementMeaning
Status, top leftSearching, Target locked, Predicting or Target lost. “Detection off” means detection is switched off. A thermometer shows that the iPhone is hot.
Format, top centreCurrent resolution and frame rate, in photo mode file type and megapixels. Tap to open the format panel.
Green frameThe target Keepsight follows. White frames are other detected subjects.
Lenses.5×, 1×, 5× and, in video mode, A for auto zoom.
ShutterRed in video mode, white in photo mode.
Switch on the leftChanges between video and photo.
Switch cameraThe round button next to the thumbnail changes between rear and front camera.
ThumbnailThe latest capture. Tap to open the gallery.
Button on the rightConnect, Track or Stop.

The three panels

  • Left, Tracking: subject, speed, gestures, voice control, countdown and the gimbal connection.
  • Right, Camera: format, auto zoom, virtual gimbal, photo trigger and overlay video.
  • Bottom, Format: resolution and frame rate for video, file type and megapixels for photos.

Open the side panels with the narrow handles at the edge of the image, by tapping or swiping. In landscape, the handle for the camera panel is a button at the top of the right column. Tap beside a panel to close it.

Choosing the target in the image

  • Tap a white frame to make that subject the target.
  • Drag a frame with your finger to follow any object, even one Keepsight does not know. This requires Keepsight Pro.

Keepsight rotates its interface when you turn the iPhone to landscape. During a video recording the image format stays fixed.
